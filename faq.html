About Noah
Noah is Baeloom's digital companion. She has her own personality and opinions, listens to what you share, remembers it over time, and helps you understand yourself more clearly. She's not a therapist and she's not a mirror. She's someone to think things through with.
No. Noah is an AI. Her responses are generated by a language model, not a human. That said, she's designed to feel like a genuine conversation: she has a consistent personality, remembers what you've shared, and engages with you honestly.
No, and she's not trying to. Baeloom is not a mental health service and Noah is not a therapist. What she can do is give you a space to think things through, especially the things that feel hard to bring up with people around you. If you're struggling, please speak to a professional.
Yes. Noah builds on what you share session by session. With Living Memory (available on Pro), she keeps track of what matters to you over time, so she can pick up where you left off and notice patterns you might not have seen yourself.
Theme rooms are dedicated spaces for a specific area of your life, things like relationships, finances, identity, or personal growth. Each room has its own ongoing conversation with Noah, so your reflections stay organized and build depth over time. Free users get 2 theme rooms; Pro users get up to 5.
Pricing & subscription
Baeloom has a free plan you can use with no credit card required. Baeloom Pro is €20 per month and unlocks everything: higher usage limits, up to 5 theme rooms, session summaries, progress tracking, image upload, and Living Memory. See our pricing page for the full breakdown.
Yes. You can cancel your subscription at any time. You'll keep access to Pro features until the end of your current billing cycle.

Payments are billed monthly starting from the day of your first successful payment. All payments are processed securely via Stripe. You won't be charged anything until you choose to upgrade.

Using the app
Head to chat.baeloom.com, create a free account, and start chatting with Noah. No credit card required.
After every session, Noah summarizes what you talked about and what changed. Every 24 hours a new summary is generated so you can track how your thinking is developing over time. This is a Pro feature.
When you reach clarity in a theme, when you've worked through it as far as you can, Noah lets you know. You'll receive an insight card that captures the pattern you uncovered, what changed, and what to do next. It lives in your theme room so you can always come back to it.
Baeloom is a web app that works fully on mobile. Visit chat.baeloom.com in your browser and add it to your home screen for the best experience. A native app is something we're currently working on.
