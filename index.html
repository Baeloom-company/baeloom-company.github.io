Before you start

The four things people ask us most.

Can Noah replace therapy?

No, and she’s not trying to. Noah is there to reflect with, not to treat. What she can do is give you a space to think things through, especially the things that feel hard to bring up with people around you. If you’re struggling, please speak to a professional.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your conversations with Noah are private and encrypted. We never use them to train AI models, and we never sell your data.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. You can cancel your subscription at any time. You’ll keep access to Pro features until the end of your current billing cycle.

What happens when my free messages run out?

You get 20 new messages every 30 days. Your conversations stay where they are. If you want more room, you can switch to Pro.
